SUPPORT

Frequently asked
questions

Does ITC provide demo-funded accounts?

No. ITC's plans are not demo-funded trading challenges. Contact us for details about account setup and services currently available.

What does profit share mean?

Gold lists a 60% profit share and Platinum lists 70%. Ask ITC how the share is calculated and paid before selecting a plan.

What does 100% loss share mean?

Both available Investor tiers show 100% loss share. Confirm in writing how losses are treated, what amount may be at risk, and the applicable terms before depositing or trading.

What is Account Maintain?

It is an account-management request for PKR 1,000 to PKR 1,000,000. Contact ITC to confirm fees, account authority, risks, and withdrawal terms. The service stays pending until ITC confirms the arrangement.

How do I ask about opening an account?

Message our team on WhatsApp to discuss the plans, account opening process, and any fees or documents required.
